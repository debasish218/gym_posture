================================================================================
KEY CONCEPTS:
================================================================================

POSE NORMALIZATION:
  The app normalizes detected poses to handle:
  - Different body sizes
  - Different camera distances
  - Different body proportions
  
  This makes alignment comparison robust across users.

ALIGNMENT SCORING:
  Each joint is compared to the reference pose.
  - Position error (distance in normalized space)
  - Angle error (joint angles like elbow, knee)
  - Overall score = average of all errors mapped to 0-100%

COLOR FEEDBACK:
  Error ≤ 15° → GREEN (excellent)
  Error ≤ 30° → YELLOW (needs work)
  Error > 30° → RED (significant correction needed)

================================================================================
TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS:
================================================================================

1. LIGHTING: Good lighting is important. Natural light or office lighting
   works well.

2. DISTANCE: Stand 2-3 feet from your camera (about arm's distance).

3. POSITION: Face the camera directly. Full body should be visible.

4. POSTURE: Start in an upright standing position. The reference pose is
   designed for upright posture.

5. ADJUSTMENTS: Read the feedback text for specific corrections. Move your
   body to align joints with the reference skeleton.

================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING:
================================================================================

Q: Camera won't open
A: Edit config.py and try changing CAMERA_INDEX = 1 or 2

Q: No pose detected
A: Increase lighting, move to 2-3 feet distance, ensure full body is visible

Q: Alignment always red
A: Adjust ALIGNMENT_THRESHOLDS in config.py (make values larger/smaller)

Q: Slow performance
A: Reduce CAMERA_WIDTH and CAMERA_HEIGHT in config.py
